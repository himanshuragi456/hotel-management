### BODIES

**Body 1 — Problem/Solution**
When a waiter writes an order by hand, three things can go wrong — wrong item, wrong quantity, lost slip. Every one of those costs you money and a bad review. Magic Management connects your waiter's phone directly to the kitchen display. The order fires the moment it's placed. No paper. No shouting. No mistakes. Your kitchen knows what to cook before the waiter even walks back.

**Body 2 — Feature Walkthrough**
Here's what happens when a table places an order on Magic Management: the waiter taps items on their phone → a KOT prints in the kitchen automatically → the chef's screen updates in real time → when the food is ready, billing gets notified → the bill is generated with GST, UPI QR, and a WhatsApp receipt in one tap. Start to finish, zero paper. Works on any phone, tablet, or laptop you already own. No new hardware.
